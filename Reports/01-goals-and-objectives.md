# 1. Goals and Objectives — Durban Youth Council (DYC) Website

## Project goal
Give DYC a professional, informative website that explains its work and turns visitors into volunteers,
partners and sponsors. DYC is an all-volunteer non-profit founded in 1966 as a Junior Chamber International
South Africa (Durban) project (Wikipedia, n.d.).

## Objectives
Each objective is specific and has a way of being measured.

| # | Objective | How success is measured |
|---|-----------|-------------------------|
| O1 | Explain who DYC is and what it does within the first screen of the home page. | Home page hero states the mission and the 1966 founding; three programmes visible without scrolling far. |
| O2 | Convert visitors into volunteer or sponsor enquiries. | Every page has a link to `enquiry.html`; the form captures name, email and interest (volunteer/sponsor). |
| O3 | Make contact details easy to find. | Both locations (Berea office, Sydenham outreach venue) shown with maps on `contact.html`; email in every footer. |
| O4 | Work on any device. | Layout adapts to desktop, tablet and mobile (Part 2). |
| O5 | Be accessible to everyone. | Semantic HTML5, skip link, alt text, visible focus states, aligned to WCAG 2.2 (W3C, 2023). |
| O6 | Be low-cost and maintainable by volunteers. | Static HTML/CSS/JS with free hosting (GitHub Docs, n.d.); first-year cash cost under R200 (see Budget). |

## Target audience
Young people (16–30) who want to volunteer, parents and schools seeking support, and businesses or
individuals considering sponsorship.

## Alignment with the project
O1–O3 are delivered by the five HTML pages built in Part 1. O4 is delivered in Part 2 (responsive CSS).
O5 and O6 are design constraints applied across all parts.
