# 10. Proposal 2 — The Baking Pan (small business) — NOT BUILT

> Details marked **[VERIFY]** must be confirmed with the business owner before this proposal is used.

| | |
|---|---|
| **Organisation** | The Baking Pan, a small bakery in Overport, Durban |
| **Type** | Small business (retail bakery) |

## 1. Goals and objectives
| # | Objective | Measure |
|---|-----------|---------|
| O1 | Show the range of baked goods | Menu page with categories and photos |
| O2 | Make ordering easy | Order/enquiry form with date and product fields |
| O3 | Help customers find the shop | Address, opening hours **[VERIFY]**, map |
| O4 | Build trust | About page and customer comments **[VERIFY permission]** |

## 2. Current website analysis
Existing web presence to be reviewed with the owner **[VERIFY]**. Typical gaps for small bakeries are no online menu,
no order route and no opening hours.

## 3. Features and functionality
Pages: Home, Menu/Products, About, Order Enquiry, Contact. Features: category menu, order form with validation,
embedded map, social links, mobile-first layout.

## 4. Design aesthetic
Warm and appetising: cream background, a rich brown for text, and a berry or golden accent. Rounded
headings font plus a readable body font. Photography of the products would be the main visual.

## 5. Wireframes
Same structure as the DYC wireframes (header, hero, card grid, form, footer); the card grid becomes a product grid.

## 6. Technical requirements
HTML5, CSS3, vanilla JavaScript, Git/GitHub, free static hosting (GitHub Docs, n.d.). Same stack as Proposal 1.

## 7. Timeline
Same three-part structure as the [DYC timeline](07-timeline.md).

## 8. Budget
Same cash costs as the [DYC budget](08-budget.md), but a **new** .co.za domain registration would be needed
(about R89 – R150 per year; Launch Llama, 2026) and professional product photography is strongly recommended
(quote required).

## Why it was not chosen
DYC had a clearer, well-researched need and could be written from verified facts, while The Baking Pan required
owner-supplied content (menu, prices, photos) that was not yet available.
