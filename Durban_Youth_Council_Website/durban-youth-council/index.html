Empowering Durban's youth since 1966.

Durban Youth Council is an all-volunteer non-profit connecting young people with the charities, schools and communities that need them most across the eThekwini metro.

Become a volunteer Learn our story

What we do

For nearly six decades, DYC volunteers have partnered with local charities and under-resourced schools to run three ongoing streams of community service.

1

School Support

Weekend tutoring, mentorship and resource drives at under-resourced primary and high schools across Durban.

2

Charity Partnerships

Hands-on volunteer days with established local charities, from care homes to shelters.

3

Youth Leadership

Workshops that build organisational and leadership skills in the volunteers themselves.
