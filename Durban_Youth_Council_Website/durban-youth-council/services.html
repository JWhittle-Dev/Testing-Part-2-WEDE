Our Programmes

Three ongoing streams of community service, run entirely by volunteers.

School Support

DYC volunteers run weekend tutoring sessions, mentorship pairings and resource drives (stationery, books, uniforms) at under-resourced primary and high schools across the Durban metro. Schools interested in a partnership can register their interest via our enquiry page.

Who it helps: learners at under-resourced primary and high schools.
When: weekends during the school term.
How to join: volunteer or register your school through the enquiry page.

Charity Partnerships

We organise hands-on volunteer days with established local charities — from care homes and shelters to environmental clean-ups — giving both first-time and experienced volunteers a structured way to contribute a Saturday morning.

Who it helps: care homes, shelters and environmental groups.
When: scheduled volunteer days, usually Saturday mornings.
How to join: sign up as a volunteer on the enquiry page.

Youth Leadership

Beyond service days, DYC runs short workshops on organisational and leadership skills, giving volunteers practical experience in planning and running community projects — skills that carry well beyond their time with the council.

Who it helps: DYC volunteers and other young people who want leadership experience.
When: short workshops scheduled through the year.
How to join: tell us your interest on the enquiry page.
