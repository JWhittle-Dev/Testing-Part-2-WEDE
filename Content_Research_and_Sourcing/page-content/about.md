# About Us (about.html) — Content

## History (sourced — see sources.md #1)
Established 1966; originally a project of Junior Chamber International
South Africa's Durban chapter. One of Durban's longest-running youth
service organisations, run entirely by volunteers.

## Mission
To empower the youth of Durban through direct community service, working
with local charities and under-resourced schools.

## Vision
A Durban where every young person has both the opportunity to serve their
community and the support of one when they need it.
