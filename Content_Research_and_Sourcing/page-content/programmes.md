# Programmes (services.html) — Content

1. **School Support** — weekend tutoring, mentorship pairings, resource
   drives (stationery, books, uniforms) at under-resourced Durban schools.
2. **Charity Partnerships** — volunteer days with established local
   charities: care homes, shelters, environmental clean-ups.
3. **Youth Leadership** — short workshops on organisational/leadership
   skills for volunteers themselves.

Sponsorship CTA: sponsorship funds transport, materials and venue costs
across all three programmes.

Note: with regards to specific charity/school partner names none are named on the site until confirmed, 
to avoid miscrediting a partnership that may not currently be active.
