## Notes on reliability
- Wikipedia is used here only for basic, low-controversy facts (founding
  year, founding body) appropriate for a class project; for a real client
  engagement these facts should be verified directly with DYC's committee
  before publishing.
- No claims about current programmes, staff, committee members, or contact
  addresses have been sourced from a verified DYC-controlled channel yet —
  these are flagged with **[VERIFY WITH ORGANISATION]** wherever they
  appear in `page-content/`, and must be confirmed before the site goes
  live for a real client.
