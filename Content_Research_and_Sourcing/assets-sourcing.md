## Fonts
| Font   | Use      | Source                                   | Licence                                     |
|--------|----------|------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Poppins| Headings | https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Poppins| Open Font License (free for commercial use) |
| Inter  | Body text| https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Inter  | Open Font License (free for commercial use) |

Both are loaded via the Google Fonts CDN link in every page's `<head>` —
no separate download needed, and both are free for commercial use with no
attribution required.

## Icons
The build uses simple numbered circles (CSS only) rather than an icon set, to avoid an extra dependency.

## Illustrations
Six original SVG illustrations and a favicon were created for the project (see `images/README.md`). No third-party artwork is used.
